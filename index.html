Our story

Built from scratch, around one stone.

JAN Moissanite was started by Malik Dawood Jan, a gemstone enthusiast, to support local craft and artisans and help their work be seen around the world.

We begin with green moissanite. It is lab-created, so nothing is mined, and many people choose it as an ethical alternative to diamond. Every piece is set in 925 sterling silver.

Details and care

Made to be worn.

Moissanite is a hard, brilliant stone, and sterling silver is a classic setting metal. A few habits keep both looking their best:

  • Wipe with a soft, dry cloth after wearing.
  • Keep away from perfume, lotions and household chemicals.
  • Store in a pouch or box, apart from other jewellery.
