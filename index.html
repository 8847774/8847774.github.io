About JAN

JAN is a new brand started by Malik Dawood Jan, a gemstone enthusiast. The goal is simple: support local craft and artisans, get their work seen around the world, and build a brand from scratch.

We begin with moissanite. It is a lab-created stone, so nothing is mined, and it is widely used as an ethical alternative to diamond. Each piece is set in 925 sterling silver.

Prices are shared by message because every stone and setting is different.
